# Product

## Register

portfolio

## Users

Hiring partners, product teams, founders, and clients evaluating Zahirul Iman's product-design, digital-product, web, and delivery work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Zahirul Iman that positions him as a Product Designer and Digital Product Builder: someone who connects ambiguous business problems, user flows, interfaces, prototypes, technical teams, and delivery.

## Brand Personality

Quietly confident, exacting, technical, and playful in small doses. The writing and product work remain the focus.

## Anti-references

Generic agency templates, oversized marketing heroes, gratuitous gradients, dense dashboards, unnecessary decoration, or interactions that obstruct reading.

## Design Principles

- Use one restrained 640px reading column.
- Let typography, spacing, and real work imagery carry the composition.
- Tell one curated story: professional work, flagship product, side business, experimentation, then photography.
- Keep copy concise and let sliders and project imagery do most of the storytelling.
- Keep interaction precise, tactile, and optional.
- Preserve the restrained light/dark visual system and direct animated theme toggle.
- Make navigation, external links, social contact icons, product imagery, and experiment controls functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic landmarks, labelled controls, keyboard-operable links and buttons, reduced-motion fallbacks, high contrast across themes, and touch-friendly targets.
